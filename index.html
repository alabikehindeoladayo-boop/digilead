The Problem

Digital exclusion is no longer just an infrastructure problem.

Before DigiLEAD, not one student at Gbenopo Community Grammar School in Bandu had ever created a digital product. Not one. There were zero functioning computers in the school. 84% of students had never built anything digital in their lives.

This is not a Bandu problem. The NCC's own data shows only 23% of rural communities across Nigeria have internet access. Just 12% of public rural schools teach computer studies at all. The Federal Government named their connectivity programme Project 774 — after every local government in Nigeria — because they know this gap is everywhere.

Geography + Poverty + Infrastructure Failure
= Total digital exclusion — for students who have exactly the same talent and potential as any other learner in this country.
